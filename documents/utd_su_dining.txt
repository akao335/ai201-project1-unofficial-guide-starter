The Student Union (SU) and Student Services Building Addition (SSA) offer several food and dining venues. Check the UT Dallas Dining website for current hours of operation, menus, specials and University meal plan meal exchange options. 


Student Union Food Court
The Student Union Food Court includes Panda Express, Crave, Kalachandji’s Express and Chick-fil-A adjacent to spacious seating areas.  



Comet Café
The Comet Café is located on the second floor of the Student Union and features Firehouse Subs, Smoothie King and Halal Shack. 



Starbucks
Starbucks inside the Student Union provides tables for enjoying your favorite coffee-infused drink or a snack, with views overlooking The Plinth. 



The Market UTD
In a hurry? Grab a snack, salad or wrap from the The Market at the Student Union or the The Market at the Student Services Building Addition.
